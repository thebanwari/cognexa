# Assignment Template

This template is used for generating assignments based on course content.

## Template Structure

### Multiple Choice Questions (MCQ)
- Format: Question with 4 options
- Focus: Key concepts and definitions
- Difficulty: Varies by course level

### Short Answer Questions
- Format: Brief explanation required
- Focus: Understanding and application
- Length: 2-3 sentences

### Long Answer Questions
- Format: Detailed explanation required
- Focus: Comprehensive understanding
- Length: 3-5 paragraphs

## Content Areas

1. **Conceptual Understanding**
   - Definitions of key terms
   - Explanation of fundamental principles
   - Relationship between concepts

2. **Practical Application**
   - Real-world examples
   - Problem-solving scenarios
   - Case studies

3. **Critical Thinking**
   - Analysis of complex topics
   - Evaluation of different approaches
   - Synthesis of multiple concepts

## Generation Guidelines

- Use course-specific terminology
- Align difficulty with course level
- Ensure questions cover all major topics
- Provide clear, unambiguous questions
- Include comprehensive answer keys
